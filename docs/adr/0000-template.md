# ADR-NNN: Title in the imperative or as a statement

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-MMM · **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

Why a decision was needed. The forces in play — technical, organisational, time. Two to five sentences.

## Decision

What we chose, in one or two sentences. Present tense: "We use…", "The agent never…".

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — why not.
- **Option B** — why not.

## Consequences

What becomes easier. What becomes harder or is now forbidden. What we will have to revisit, and the signal that tells us to.
